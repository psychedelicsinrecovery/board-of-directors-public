# 🏛️ PIR® Board of Directors

> 🌐 [Board of Directors page](https://service.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/board-of-directors/) ·
> 🎫 Bring something to the Board: type `/board` in [PIR®'s Discord](https://discord.gg/MyprTq8w95) or use the
> [web helpdesk](https://psychedelicsinrecovery.github.io/alex-desk-public/portal.html#board)

**Psychedelics In Recovery™ (PIR®)** is a fellowship and a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Its **Board of Directors** is a small
group of trusted servants who look after the things a fellowship can't leave to chance: PIR®'s finances and filings,
its legal and governance health, its trademarks, and its long-range plans. The Board serves the fellowship; group
conscience and the Traditions come first.

## 🪑 Who serves
Three officers (🏛️ President, 🖋️ Secretary, 💰 Treasurer) and four Members-at-Large, each with its own area of
service. Current seats, open seats and elections are on the
[🏛️ Board of Directors page](https://service.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/board-of-directors/), and PIR®'s trusted
servants are in [📇 CONTACTS.md](https://github.com/psychedelicsinrecovery/.github/blob/main/CONTACTS.md).

## 📨 How to bring something to the Board
Any service member or committee can send the Board a proposal, a question or a request.

| | How |
|---|---|
| 💬 **In Discord** | Type `/board`. Pick *idea / feedback* for a proposal, or *problem / request* for anything that needs a decision. A private thread opens with you and the Board. |
| 🌐 **On the web** | The [web helpdesk](https://psychedelicsinrecovery.github.io/alex-desk-public/portal.html#board) → **BoardDesk**. Sign in with a one-time email link; no Discord needed. |
| 🗂️ **From a committee** | Draft it in your committee's private repo under `proposals/` (work in progress goes in `proposals/drafts/`). Finished proposals are collected into the Board's private inbox automatically every night. |

Either way, it lands in one place, so nothing gets lost in a chat thread between meetings.

## 🔄 What happens next
1. 📥 The Secretary logs it in the Board's private index and adds it to an upcoming agenda.
2. 💬 The Board discusses it, and may invite you to the meeting or ask questions in your thread.
3. 🗳️ The decision is recorded as a resolution, and you hear back in the same thread.
4. 📣 Anything the Board chooses to share publicly appears in [`published/`](published/).

💰 Money matters (spending, donations, holdings) are decided by the Board with the Treasurer's advice. Nobody else
can commit PIR®'s funds.

## 🪞 Public mirror
This repository is a **filtered mirror** of the Board's private working repo, published automatically by an allowlist
(only this README and `published/`). Proposals, briefs, minutes, resolutions and anything with personal details stay
private. Commits here keep their original authors, because the mirror is made by filtering the private history rather
than by copying files. Please don't open pull requests here; use `/board` instead.
